Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This page explains what data Speak With Confidence AI collects and how it's used.

What we store on our side

The situations you describe are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate talking points. We do not store these descriptions on our own servers after the request completes.

What's stored in your browser

Your access code, saved phrases, checklist progress, and practice count are stored locally in your browser (localStorage). This data never leaves your device unless you clear your browser data — we don't have access to it, and it isn't synced anywhere.

Third parties

We use Anthropic (Claude API) to generate talking points, and Lava.top to process payment. Each has its own privacy policy governing the data it handles.

No accounts, no tracking

Speak With Confidence AI doesn't require an account, doesn't use analytics cookies to track you across sites, and doesn't sell any data.
