What to say in your performance review

A performance review is your chance to show your impact and shape the next year. These lines help you present results, respond to criticism and ask for growth.

Three things that help most
  • Bring a short list of results with numbers.
  • Ask what "exceeds expectations" looks like for your role.
  • Agree on two or three goals and how they will be measured.

40 phrases you can use

  • I'd like to start by going over what I achieved this year.
  • Here are my three biggest results, with the numbers behind them.
  • I'm proud of this project, and here's why it mattered.
  • Where do you think I could improve?
  • I'd like to grow into a lead role. What would that take?
  • I set myself these goals at the start of the year. Here's how I did.
  • This goal wasn't met, and here's what got in the way.
  • What would make next year a great year for me in your eyes?
  • I'd like more opportunities to work directly with clients.
  • Could we agree on clear goals for the next six months?
  • I'd like to talk about my career path, not just this year.
  • I'd appreciate more regular feedback, maybe once a month.
  • I took on extra work when the team was short, and I'd like that recognized.
  • I want to be honest about what was hard this year.
  • I'd like training in project management. Is there budget for that?
  • How do my results compare with what you expected?
  • What's the most important thing for me to focus on next?
  • I'd like to understand how this rating was decided.
  • I respectfully disagree with this part of the review.
  • Here's some feedback from clients I worked with.
