How to make a complaint that gets results

Effective complaints are polite, factual and clear about the fix you want. These lines help you describe the problem, stay calm and get a refund, repair or replacement.

Three things that help most
  • Stick to facts, dates and what was promised.
  • Say exactly what would resolve it for you.
  • If nothing changes, ask politely to speak to someone who can decide.

40 phrases you can use

  • I'd like to report a problem with my apartment.
  • The heating hasn't worked for three days.
  • I've already reported this twice. When will it be fixed?
  • I'd like to speak to someone who can make a decision.
  • I'm not happy with the service I received today.
  • The food was cold, and we waited forty minutes.
  • Could you take this off the bill, please?
  • I'd like to make a complaint about one of your staff.
  • I was charged for something I didn't order.
  • The neighbors are very loud after midnight.
  • Could you please keep the noise down after eleven?
  • I'd like this resolved by the end of the week.
  • I'm putting this in writing, so there's a record.
  • I'm a regular customer, and this was disappointing.
  • What will you do to make sure it doesn't happen again?
  • I'd like compensation for the inconvenience.
  • The repair was done badly and needs to be redone.
  • I booked a room with a sea view, and this isn't one.
  • I'd like a different room, please.
  • Could I have the name of the person I'm speaking to?
