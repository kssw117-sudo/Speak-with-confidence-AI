How to respond to feedback, even when it stings

How you react to feedback shapes how much people trust you. These phrases help you listen without getting defensive, ask clarifying questions and agree on next steps.

Three things that help most
  • Thank them first, even if you disagree.
  • Ask for one concrete example if the feedback is vague.
  • Take a day before responding to feedback that feels unfair.

40 phrases you can use

  • Thank you for telling me. I'd rather know than not know.
  • Can you give me a specific example, so I understand exactly what you mean?
  • That's hard to hear, but I think you're right.
  • Let me think about this before I respond.
  • I hadn't seen it that way. Thank you for pointing it out.
  • What would you like me to do differently next time?
  • I appreciate you being direct with me.
  • I understand the concern. Here's the context behind my decision.
  • I don't fully agree, but I want to understand your view better.
  • Could we agree on how we'll measure improvement?
  • I'll work on this and check back with you in two weeks.
  • Is this something others have noticed too?
  • I want to get better at this. Do you have any resources you'd recommend?
  • That's fair. I'll own it.
  • I'm a bit surprised, because I thought it went well. Can you tell me more?
  • What did I do well that I should keep doing?
  • I'd like to respond calmly, so give me a moment.
  • I see now how that came across. It wasn't my intention.
  • Can I ask a few questions to make sure I've got it right?
  • I'll take that on board.
