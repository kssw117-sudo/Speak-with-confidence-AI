Networking and small talk phrases

Small talk opens doors when you have a few easy lines ready. These phrases help you start a conversation, keep it going, leave it gracefully and follow up.

Three things that help most
  • Ask open questions about their work and really listen.
  • Have a one-sentence answer ready for "what do you do?"
  • Follow up within 48 hours with something useful.

40 phrases you can use

  • What's been the most interesting part of your work lately?
  • How did you end up in this field?
  • What brings you to this event?
  • I don't think we've met. I'm Alex, nice to meet you.
  • How do you know the host?
  • What are you working on these days that you're excited about?
  • That sounds fascinating. How did you get started with it?
  • I've heard a lot about your company. What's it like working there?
  • What's the biggest challenge in your industry right now?
  • I'm new to this field, so I'm here to learn. What would you recommend I read?
  • Is this your first time at this conference?
  • Which talk did you enjoy the most today?
  • I loved what you said in the panel about remote teams.
  • I work in product design. What about you?
  • Do you mind if I join you?
  • It's been great talking to you. Can I connect with you online?
  • I'd love to continue this conversation. Could we grab a coffee next week?
  • Let me introduce you to someone you should meet.
  • I don't want to keep you, but I'm glad we got to talk.
  • What kind of projects are you looking for right now?
