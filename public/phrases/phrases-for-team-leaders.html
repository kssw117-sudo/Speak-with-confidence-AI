Phrases for leading a team

New and experienced leaders both need clear words for setting direction, delegating, recognizing good work and handling problems. These lines help you lead with clarity and warmth.

Three things that help most
  • Explain the why behind a task, not just the what.
  • Recognize good work in public and correct in private.
  • Ask "what do you need from me?" in every one-to-one.

40 phrases you can use

  • Here's where we're going, and here's why it matters.
  • I trust you to make this call.
  • What do you need from me to get this done?
  • Let's celebrate this win before we move on.
  • I'd like everyone's honest view, especially if you disagree.
  • I made a mistake in planning this, and I'm sorry.
  • Our priority this week is the launch. Everything else can wait.
  • Who has capacity to take this on?
  • I'm going to make a decision now, and we can review it later.
  • I don't have all the answers, but we'll figure it out together.
  • Thank you for staying late. It didn't go unnoticed.
  • Let's agree on how we communicate when things go wrong.
  • I'll protect the team's time from extra requests.
  • This is a stretch goal, but I think we can do it.
  • What's blocking you right now?
  • I'd like each of you to own one area.
  • Let's keep meetings short and focused.
  • I want to hear bad news early, not late.
  • That's a great idea. Can you lead it?
  • We're behind schedule. Here's the plan to catch up.
