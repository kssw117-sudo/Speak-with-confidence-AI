What to say in your first weeks at a new job

The first weeks set how colleagues see you. These lines help you introduce yourself, ask questions without feeling silly, learn how things work and start contributing.

Three things that help most
  • Ask your manager what a great first three months looks like.
  • Meet the key people one to one in your first two weeks.
  • Write down what you learn, and share it with the next new person.

39 phrases you can use

  • Hi, I'm new here. I just joined the marketing team.
  • It's great to finally meet you in person.
  • Could you tell me a bit about what you work on?
  • I'm still learning how things work here, so thanks for your patience.
  • I'd like to understand how my role fits into the bigger picture.
  • What does a great first three months look like in this role?
  • Is there anything I should read before my first project?
  • How does the team prefer to communicate, chat, email or meetings?
  • I'll probably ask a lot of questions in the first few weeks.
  • Let me check that I understood the process correctly.
  • Could you walk me through how this was done last time?
  • I noticed something that might be improved. Can I share an idea?
  • I don't want to change things too fast. I'm just curious why it works this way.
  • Thank you for showing me around. That was really helpful.
  • Could we set up a short weekly check-in while I settle in?
  • What are the biggest challenges the team is facing right now?
  • Who are the key people I should get to know?
  • I'd love to grab a coffee with you sometime this week.
  • In my last job I worked on something similar, so I may be able to help.
  • I'm happy to take on a small task to get started.
