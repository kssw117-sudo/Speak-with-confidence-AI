How to handle conflict with a colleague

Unresolved tension with a coworker drains energy every day. These phrases help you raise the issue privately, listen to their side and agree on how to work together.

Three things that help most
  • Talk in private and soon, before it grows.
  • Describe the situation and its effect, not their character.
  • Ask for their view before proposing a solution.

39 phrases you can use

  • Can we talk for a few minutes? Something's been bothering me.
  • I'd like to clear the air between us.
  • I don't think we're on the same page, and I want to fix that.
  • When the meeting moved without me, I felt left out.
  • Help me understand your side of this.
  • I may have misread the situation. Can you tell me what happened?
  • I don't want this to affect our work.
  • Let's focus on the problem, not on each other.
  • I felt my idea was dismissed earlier. Can we talk about it?
  • I'd appreciate it if you didn't take credit for my work.
  • I know we both want the project to succeed.
  • What would make working together easier for you?
  • Can we agree on how we'll split the work from now on?
  • I need you to copy me on emails about my project.
  • I'd prefer to hear concerns directly, not through others.
  • I think there's been a misunderstanding.
  • Let's find a solution that works for both of us.
  • I respect your experience, and I'd like you to respect mine.
  • I'm not trying to win this argument. I'm trying to fix it.
  • Can we take a break and continue this tomorrow?
