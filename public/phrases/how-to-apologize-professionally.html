How to apologize well, at work and in life

A good apology is short, specific and includes a fix. These lines help you take responsibility without over-explaining or blaming others.

Three things that help most
  • Name exactly what went wrong.
  • Skip "but" and long excuses.
  • Say what you will do differently, and then do it.

40 phrases you can use

  • I'm sorry. I got that wrong, and I understand why you're upset.
  • I should have told you sooner, and I didn't. That's on me.
  • I'm sorry for the delay. Here's what I'm doing to fix it.
  • I'm not going to make excuses. I made a mistake.
  • I hurt you, and I'm truly sorry.
  • I apologize for my tone earlier. It wasn't fair to you.
  • Thank you for telling me how that felt.
  • What can I do to make this right?
  • I understand if you need some time.
  • I'm sorry I missed the deadline. I've set up a reminder so it won't happen again.
  • I wasn't listening properly, and I'm sorry.
  • I was wrong, and you were right.
  • I'm sorry for the confusion. Let me clarify.
  • I should have checked before I promised.
  • I take full responsibility for what happened.
  • I'm sorry this affected your work.
  • I can't undo it, but I can make sure it doesn't happen again.
  • I'm sorry I interrupted you. Please go on.
  • I understand why you lost trust in me, and I want to earn it back.
  • I'm sorry I forgot. It matters to me, and I should have remembered.
