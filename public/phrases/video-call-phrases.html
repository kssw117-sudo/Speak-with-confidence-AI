Useful phrases for remote work and video calls

Remote work needs extra-clear communication. These lines help you run smooth calls, deal with tech problems, set boundaries on availability and keep your work visible.

Three things that help most
  • Send a short written summary after every important call.
  • Agree on response times so people know when to expect a reply.
  • Make your progress visible in shared tools instead of waiting to be asked.

40 phrases you can use

  • Can everyone hear me okay?
  • Sorry, you're on mute.
  • I think you froze for a second. Could you repeat the last part?
  • My connection is a bit unstable, so I'll turn off my camera for now.
  • Let me share my screen. Can you see it?
  • I'll drop the link in the chat.
  • Sorry, I was talking over you. Please go ahead.
  • Before we start, does anyone need to leave early?
  • Let's give people one more minute to join.
  • I'll keep this short so we can all get back to work.
  • I'll send a summary in writing right after the call.
  • Could we switch this to a quick call? It's hard to explain in chat.
  • I'm working from a different time zone, so mornings are best for me.
  • I'm usually online from nine to five, Central European Time.
  • I'll be offline for the next two hours, but I'll reply after that.
  • Let's not set a meeting for this. A short message will do.
  • Can we record this call for the people who couldn't make it?
  • I'd like to hear from people who haven't spoken yet.
  • I'll stop sharing now so we can see each other.
  • There's a bit of an echo. Could you check your microphone?
