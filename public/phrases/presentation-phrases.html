Useful phrases for presentations and public speaking

Strong presenters use simple signposts that keep the audience with them. These lines help you open, move between sections, handle questions and close with impact.

Three things that help most
  • Tell the audience what they will get in the first 30 seconds.
  • Use a clear transition line between sections.
  • If you do not know an answer, say you will follow up, and do it.

40 phrases you can use

  • Good morning, everyone. Thank you for being here.
  • I'll keep this to fifteen minutes, with time for questions at the end.
  • Here's the one thing I'd like you to remember today.
  • Let me start with a quick story.
  • By the end of this talk, you'll know three ways to save time.
  • Let me show you the numbers first.
  • This slide shows where we were a year ago.
  • Here's what changed, and why it matters.
  • I'll pause here for a second. Any questions so far?
  • That's a great question. Let me answer it directly.
  • I don't know the answer to that, but I'll find out and follow up.
  • Let me come back to that question at the end.
  • To sum up, there are three main points.
  • Here's what I'm asking you to do next.
  • Thank you for your attention.
  • I see some nodding, so let me move on.
  • Bear with me while I find the right slide.
  • Let me put that in simpler terms.
  • Imagine you're a customer opening our app for the first time.
  • The biggest risk is not what you'd expect.
