Phrases for calling customer service

Calls to banks, airlines and providers go better when you are calm and clear. These lines help you explain the problem, ask for what you need and escalate if necessary.

Three things that help most
  • Have your account number and dates ready before you call.
  • Say what outcome you want in one sentence.
  • Ask for a reference number and the agent's name.

40 phrases you can use

  • Hi, I'm calling about my order from last week.
  • My order number is on the confirmation email. Would you like me to read it?
  • The item arrived damaged. What are my options?
  • I'd like a refund, please.
  • Could you explain why I was charged twice?
  • I've been waiting for two weeks. When will it arrive?
  • I'd like to cancel my subscription.
  • Can I speak to a manager, please?
  • I understand it's not your fault, and I appreciate your help.
  • Could you put that in writing for me?
  • What's the reference number for this call?
  • I've already explained this twice. Can you check the notes?
  • That's not what I was told last time.
  • What exactly will happen next, and when?
  • Could you call me back if we get cut off?
  • I'd like to change my delivery address.
  • The product doesn't work as described.
  • I'm not happy with this solution. What else can you offer?
  • Can you check if there's a discount available?
  • I'd like to return this item. How does that work?
