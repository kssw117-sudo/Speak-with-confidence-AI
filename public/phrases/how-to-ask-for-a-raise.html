What to say when you ask for a raise

Asking for more money feels awkward because most of us never practiced it. These lines help you open the conversation calmly, make your case with results, name a number and handle a "not yet" without losing your nerve.

Three things that help most
  • Lead with two or three results that have numbers, not with how long you have worked there.
  • Say your number or range once, then stop talking and let them answer.
  • If the answer is no, ask what would need to happen and agree on a date to revisit it.

40 phrases you can use

  • I'd like to talk about my compensation given what I've delivered this year.
  • Based on my research, the market rate for this role is higher than my current pay.
  • I'm not looking for a number right now, I want to understand what a path to the next level looks like.
  • I want to make sure I'm being paid in line with my impact, not just my tenure.
  • I've taken on more responsibility since my last review, and I'd like that reflected in my pay.
  • What would need to be true for us to revisit this in three months?
  • I'm not comparing myself to anyone else on the team, just to what this role is worth.
  • Thank you for making time for this. I want to talk about my salary.
  • Over the last year I led two projects that came in early and under budget.
  • I'd like to propose an increase to this figure, based on the results I've brought in.
  • I've looked at salary data for similar roles in our area, and I'm currently below the range.
  • I enjoy working here and I want to keep growing with the company.
  • Can we talk about what a fair adjustment would look like?
  • If a raise isn't possible right now, can we agree on a timeline for when it will be?
  • I understand budgets are tight. Is there flexibility in other parts of the package?
  • Would you be open to discussing extra paid time off or a training budget instead?
  • I'd appreciate it if you could take this to whoever makes the final decision.
  • What specific goals would I need to hit to earn this increase?
  • Could we put those goals in writing, so we're both clear on what success looks like?
  • I'm asking because I want to stay, not because I'm planning to leave.
