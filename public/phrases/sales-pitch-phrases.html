Sales and pitch phrases that do not sound pushy

Good selling is mostly asking good questions and listening. These lines help you open a call, uncover needs, present value and handle objections without pressure.

Three things that help most
  • Ask about their problem before you present your solution.
  • Answer objections with a question to understand them first.
  • Always end with a clear next step and a date.

40 phrases you can use

  • Thanks for taking the call. I'll keep it short.
  • Can I ask what's the biggest challenge you're facing right now?
  • What are you currently using to solve this?
  • How much time does your team spend on this each week?
  • Here's how we helped a business like yours.
  • Would it help if I showed you a quick example?
  • What would need to be true for this to be a yes?
  • Who else would be involved in this decision?
  • I understand. What's behind that concern?
  • That's a common question. Here's how it works.
  • If it doesn't work for you, you can cancel anytime.
  • What's your timeline for making a decision?
  • Is budget the main concern, or is it something else?
  • I'd suggest starting with a small pilot.
  • Let me send you a short summary and a few examples.
  • Can we book a follow-up call for next week?
  • What would success look like for you in three months?
  • I don't want to sell you something you don't need.
  • We're not the cheapest, but here's why customers stay with us.
  • Here's what you get on day one.
