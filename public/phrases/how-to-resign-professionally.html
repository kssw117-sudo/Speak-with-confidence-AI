How to resign professionally

How you leave matters as much as how you start. These lines help you tell your manager, handle a counteroffer and leave on good terms.

Three things that help most
  • Tell your manager in person or on a call before you send the letter.
  • Keep your reasons short and positive.
  • Offer a clear handover plan for your last weeks.

40 phrases you can use

  • Thank you for meeting with me. I've decided to resign.
  • This wasn't an easy decision, and I'm grateful for my time here.
  • My last day will be four weeks from today.
  • I've accepted an offer that fits where I want to go next.
  • I want to leave things in the best possible shape.
  • I'll prepare a handover document for whoever takes over.
  • I'm happy to help train my replacement.
  • I've learned so much here, especially from you.
  • My decision is final, but I appreciate the counteroffer.
  • I'd rather not go into detail about the new company yet.
  • I'll make sure all my projects are documented before I go.
  • Can we agree on how to tell the team?
  • I'd like to stay in touch after I leave.
  • I'm leaving for the role, not because of any one person.
  • If it helps, I can stay an extra week to finish the project.
  • I'd be happy to give honest feedback in an exit interview.
  • Thank you for the opportunities you gave me.
  • I'll hand over all my accounts and passwords properly.
  • I'm sorry for the timing. I know this quarter is busy.
  • I'd appreciate a reference when the time comes.
