What to say to your landlord about rent, repairs and deposits

Renting goes smoother when you know how to ask clearly. These lines help you view a flat, report repairs, discuss rent increases, get your deposit back and talk to neighbors.

Three things that help most
  • Report repairs in writing, with photos and dates.
  • Take photos of the flat on the day you move in.
  • Ask about the notice period before you sign.

40 phrases you can use

  • Hi, I'm calling about the apartment listed online. Is it still available?
  • Could I come and see it this week?
  • How much is the rent, and what's included?
  • Are utilities included, or do I pay them separately?
  • How much is the deposit, and when do I get it back?
  • Is the contract for a fixed term, or month to month?
  • Are pets allowed in the building?
  • I'd like to take some photos of the apartment before I move in.
  • I've noticed some damage, and I'd like it written in the contract.
  • The heating isn't working. When can someone come to fix it?
  • There's a leak under the kitchen sink. It's getting worse.
  • The washing machine has stopped working.
  • Could you send someone to check the electricity? The lights keep going off.
  • I reported this problem two weeks ago, and it still isn't fixed.
  • I'd appreciate a date for the repair, please.
  • Is it okay if I let the repair person in while you're away?
  • I'll be home on Tuesday after four. Does that work?
  • Can I paint the walls if I return them to the original color?
  • I'd like to renew the lease for another year.
  • I'm planning to move out at the end of next month.
