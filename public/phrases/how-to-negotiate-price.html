Phrases for negotiating price and terms

Whether you sell a service or buy one, negotiation is a conversation, not a fight. These lines help you anchor your price, respond to discounts and trade concessions instead of giving them away.

Three things that help most
  • Never give a discount without getting something back, such as a longer contract or faster payment.
  • Answer "that's too expensive" with a question about what matters most to them.
  • Put the agreed terms in writing the same day.

40 phrases you can use

  • Thank you for the quote. Is there any flexibility on the price?
  • That's above our budget. What could you offer within this range?
  • If we commit to a longer contract, could you lower the monthly rate?
  • What's included in this price, and what costs extra?
  • I'd like to understand how you arrived at this number.
  • We've received a lower quote elsewhere, but we'd prefer to work with you.
  • Can we split the payment into two parts?
  • I can accept that price if delivery is within two weeks.
  • What would make this work for both of us?
  • I'm not able to go that low, but I can meet you halfway.
  • My rate reflects the quality and speed I deliver.
  • I can reduce the scope to fit your budget.
  • That's my best offer for this project.
  • Let's put the terms in writing before we start.
  • I need fifty percent upfront, and the rest on delivery.
  • Could we agree on a fixed price instead of hourly?
  • If anything changes in scope, we'll agree on a new price first.
  • I'd like to think about it and come back to you tomorrow.
  • Is that your final offer?
  • What's most important to you here, price, speed or quality?
