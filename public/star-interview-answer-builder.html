How the STAR method works

PartWhat to sayShare
SituationSet the scene in one or two sentences.~15%
TaskYour responsibility or the goal.~10%
ActionWhat you did, step by step. The main part.~55%
ResultThe outcome, ideally with numbers, and what you learned.~20%

A strong answer usually takes 1.5 to 2 minutes to say. Much shorter feels thin; much longer loses the interviewer.

FAQ

How long should a STAR answer be?About 1.5 to 2 minutes spoken, roughly 200 to 300 words.

What if my result wasn't a success?That's fine. Say what you learned and what you'd do differently. Interviewers value honest reflection.

Should I say “we” or “I”?Use “I” for your own actions. Interviewers want to know what you personally did.
